Terms of Service

Last updated 21 November 2026.

These terms are the rules for using Sakerhet. By adding the bot to a server or using its commands, you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use the bot.

The short version: Be kind, follow Discord's rules, and know that the bot is free, still being built, and comes with no promises.

About the bot

Sakerhet is a free and open source Discord bot made by kynorie. The code is on GitHub.

Using Sakerhet

  • You must follow the Discord Terms of Service and the Discord Community Guidelines.
  • You must be old enough to use Discord.
  • Do not use the bot to break the law, harass people, or hurt others.
  • Do not try to break the bot, attack it, or get around its limits.
  • Do not use the bot to collect other people's personal information.

Server owners and admins

If you add the bot to a server, you are in charge of how it is used there. Only admins can use the admin commands. You are responsible for your server's rules and for how you use /lockdown, /unlock and /config. We suggest you tell your members that the bot is in your server and that it can delete messages and kick people.

Automatic actions

Raid protection runs by itself. It can delete messages, kick people and lock your server. It can make mistakes, such as kicking someone who did nothing wrong. We are not responsible for these mistakes. Admins can change how strict the bot is with /config.

Anonymous messages

  • You are responsible for every message you send with /anonsend.
  • Do not send threats, harassment, hate, spam, illegal content, or private information about other people.
  • Anonymous messages are saved without your user ID, so we cannot tell who sent one. We may remove messages that break these terms.

Bulk delete

  • Deleted messages cannot be brought back. Be sure before you run /bulkdelete.
  • Keep your password safe. We cannot read or recover it.
  • Discord limits how fast bots can delete messages, so it can take a long time.

Blocked links

The bot uses public block lists and the bot owner's own list. These lists are not perfect. The bot may block a link it should not, or miss a link it should block.

No promises

Sakerhet is free and comes as it is, with no promises. It is still being built. It may be offline, have bugs, or change at any time. We may add, change or remove features without notice.

What we are not responsible for

As far as the law allows, we are not responsible for any loss or harm from using the bot. This includes deleted messages, kicked members, lost data, and times when the bot is offline.

Stopping

You can stop using Sakerhet at any time by removing the bot from your server. We can stop a person or a server from using the bot if they break these terms.

Donations

Donations are optional. They do not buy special features or special treatment, and they cannot be refunded.

Your data

Read the privacy policy to learn what the bot stores and how to delete it with /forgetme.

Open source

The code is open source and has its own license on GitHub. These terms are about using the bot that we run.

Law

These terms follow the law of England and Wales. The courts of England and Wales can deal with any dispute about them.

Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we will change the date at the top of this page. If you keep using the bot after a change, it means you accept the new terms.
